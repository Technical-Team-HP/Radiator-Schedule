How it's calculated

Mean water temperature (MWT) = flow temperature − half the flow/return difference. The flow/return difference is 5K up to 50°C and 10K at 51°C and above, so that's flow − 2.5 or flow − 5. The flow and outdoor design temperatures come from the uploaded schedule and can't be edited.

Needs @ΔT50 is the radiator rating a room needs to cover its heat loss: heat loss ÷ ((MWT − room temp) ÷ 50)1.3, rounded down (BTU = W × 3.41).

Customer-supplied radiators are entered at their ΔT50 rating and converted to output at design conditions with the same curve. Existing and proposed outputs are used as stated on the schedule.

Coverage = total radiator output in the room ÷ room heat loss. Where coverage is 97% or more, the estimated room temperature is the design temperature. Below 97%, it is where radiator output and room heat loss balance at the outdoor design temperature (exponent 1.3).
